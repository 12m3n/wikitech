WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT 
SPECIFICATION & ARCHITECTURE 
BRIEF 
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & CORE SCOPE 
The primary objective of this project is to develop a high-performance, 
enterprise-grade digital ecosystem that serves as the central hub for our multi
disciplinary professional services. The website must bridge the gap between 
technical IT infrastructure and high-level financial consulting. 
1. Brand Authority & Trust: Establish a premium digital presence that signals 
capability to Enterprise, Mid-Market, and SME clients across global 
jurisdictions. 
2. Lead Generation & Conversion: Implement strategic Call-to-Actions (CTAs) 
and an interactive quote builder to capture high-intent inbound leads for 
ERP and Tax services. 
3. Information Architecture: Provide a logical, frictionless navigation 
experience for diverse user personas (e.g., a CTO looking for SolarWinds 
vs. a CFO looking for Tax Compliance). 
4. Integration Efficiency: Automate lead flow by connecting website forms 
directly to internal CRM and project management systems. 
5. Technical Excellence: Ensure 99.9% uptime, sub-1.5s load times, and a 
mobile-first responsive architecture. 
2. NUMBERED MASTER SERVICE CATALOG 
(WHAT WE DO) 
The developer must ensure that each of the following service pillars is 
represented with specific metadata and structured data for SEO: 
1. Tier-1 Enterprise ERP Implementations: Specialized deployment and 
optimization of SAP (S/4HANA, Business One) and Oracle Cloud ERP. 
2. Mid-Market Cloud ERP Systems: Agile implementations for growing 
organizations using NetSuite, Odoo, and ERPNext. 
3. SME & Cloud Accounting Systems: Transitioning small businesses to 
automated platforms like QuickBooks Online (QBO), Zoho Books, and Xero. 
4. End-to-End Process Implementation: Comprehensive consulting covering 
requirements blueprinting, business process re-engineering (BPR), data 
migration, user training, and hypercare support. 
5. SolarWinds & Industrial IT Infrastructure: Technical solutions featuring 
SolarWinds Network Performance Monitor (NPM), Server & Application 
Monitor (SAM), Large Industry Network Solutions, and OT/IT bridging. 
6. Global Accounting & Bookkeeping Outsourcing: Scalable offshore 
accounting pods, full-cycle bookkeeping, monthly closes, and 
administrative AP/AR management. 
7. Business Planning & Forecasting: Advanced financial modeling, cash flow 
modeling, budget forecasting, and sensitivity analysis. 
8. Multi-Jurisdictional Tax Compliance: 
○ Pakistan Taxation: FBR income tax, sales tax on services (PRA, SRB, 
KPRA), and withholding tax management under Income Tax 
Ordinance 2001. 
○ UK Taxation: HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliance, VAT 
returns, and Corporation Tax. 
○ US Taxation: Federal & State compliance, state sales tax (CDTFA), 
and 1099 reporting. 
9. E-Commerce & Retail Integrations: Direct synchronization of Shopify, 
WooCommerce, Amazon, and Magento with ERP/Accounting backends 
(QBO, NetSuite, Odoo, SAP). 
10. Agentic AI & Custom Software: Development of autonomous AI agents, 
invoice OCR solutions, custom middleware, and client-facing dashboards. 
11. Marketing Solutions: Integrated B2B lead generation funnels, Meta Ads, 
and Google Ads management synchronized with client CRMs. 
3. WEBSITE SITEMAP & PAGE-BY-PAGE 
STRUCTURE 
1. Home Page: High-impact hero section with value proposition; "Service 
Selection" grid; "Why Choose Us" section; featured client logos; and links 
to core service pillars. 
2. ERP & Business Software Page: Features an Interactive System Selector 
allowing users to filter solutions based on company size (SME/Enterprise) 
and industry. Specific sub-sections for SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Odoo, 
ERPNext, QBO, and Zoho. 
3. IT Infrastructure & SolarWinds Page: Technical focus. Includes monitoring 
solution breakdowns, industrial network diagrams (iconography), and 
SolarWinds specific module listings. 
4. Global Accounting & Outsourcing Page: Focus on "Offshore Efficiency." 
Detailed breakdown of bookkeeping cycles, monthly reporting packages, 
and the "Accounting Pod" model. 
5. Tax Compliance Page: A three-pillar layout (Pakistan, UK, US) detailing 
specific regional regulations and our compliance methodology for each. 
6. E-Commerce Integration Page: Visual representation of data flow between 
e-commerce platforms (Shopify/Amazon) and ERP back-ends. 
7. Agent AI & Custom Software Page: Portfolio-style display of AI agents, 
middleware capabilities, and API connectivity features. 
8. Business Planning & Forecasting Page: Showcasing financial model 
samples, forecasting dashboards, and strategic advisory services. 
9. About & Case Studies Page: Team background, mission statement, and a 
searchable gallery of success stories filtered by service type. 
10. Interactive Quote Builder & Booking Page: A multi-step form that 
generates a preliminary service estimate and integrates with a calendar 
(e.g., Calendly) for instant consultation booking.
